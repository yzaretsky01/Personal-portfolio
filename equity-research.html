The companies

Micron Technology MU

  • Designs and manufactures memory and storage chips, sold to companies that build them into finished products.
  • Two product lines: DRAM (short-term memory) and NAND flash (long-term storage).
  • Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron together hold 95% of the DRAM market.

SanDisk SNDK

  • Sells NAND flash storage across three segments: enterprise and data center, client/OEM, and consumer retail.
  • NAND market share: Samsung 29%, SK Hynix 18%, Kioxia 14%, Micron 13%, SanDisk / Western Digital 13%, YMTC 13%.

Palantir PLTR

  • Sells enterprise software platforms through subscriptions and contracts. The platforms integrate an organization's scattered data into one system, then add AI tools on top for analysis and decision-making.
  • Serves government and commercial customers.

Recent stock performance

Micron

  • Became the latest U.S. company to reach a $1 trillion market cap.
  • Shares up 241% year to date, driven by demand for DRAM, NAND and high-bandwidth memory for AI.
  • Down 28.3% over the last four weeks on Chinese competition and weaker confidence in AI spending (Yahoo Finance).
  • Positive: a strategic agreement with Anthropic to scale AI infrastructure.

SanDisk

  • Like Micron, rose sharply over the past year.
  • Down 46.06% over the last four weeks.
  • Chinese chipmaker CXMT went public at a $500 billion market cap and raised $8 billion to expand production. That supply could end the global memory shortage that Micron and SanDisk have benefited from.

Palantir

  • Shares down 25% year to date, but up 6.7% over the last four weeks.
  • A strong first quarter brought in $1.6 billion in revenue. Several analysts backed the stock ahead of Q2 earnings on August 3.
